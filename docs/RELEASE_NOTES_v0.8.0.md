# Modifier v0.8.0 - Facilities

Requires game beta.33+.

## New
- **Facilities** (Club hub -> Facilities tab). Eight buildings with three levels each: Academy Campus, Scouting Network, Wellbeing Centre, Analytics Lab, Arena, Fan Zone, Streaming Studio (Trophy Hall comes later). They need a minimum headquarters tier, cost money up front and monthly upkeep, and take days to build (two builds at once).
- **Unlock:** facilities open once your club has won **2 majors and 5 opens** in total (counted from the moment you play with v0.8.0), as a Tier A manager.
- **Difficulty mode** (Relaxed / Standard / Ruthless), chosen once, locked after your first build.
- **Update popup:** the Modifier now tells you when a newer version exists.
- Texts of the Facilities screen live in `src/ui/late-texts.js` (editable).

## Not in this version (kept in the plan)
Rival hunger, crises, feeder clubs, hosting, legacy, manager offers.
