# Modifier v0.11.1 - Staff screen and a better start for weak Tier B clubs

Requires game beta.33+. Builds on v0.11.0.

## New: Staff tab on the Scouting screen (Transfers -> Scouts)
- The Scouting screen has a Scouting | Staff tab bar. On Staff you see your scouts (skill, weekly wage, available or out on a job) and the scouts looking for work this season.
- **Hire** a scout if the wage fits in your weekly wage budget (up to 3 scouts). **Fire** a scout who is not out on a job. A Rookie ($100/wk) is cheap and slow, a Veteran ($400/wk) costs more and finishes reports sooner.
- The number of scouts is the number of scouting jobs that can run at once. The "x of 3 scouts out" text now follows your real scout count.
- v0.11.0 listed hiring and firing, but it had no screen. This is that screen.

## Changed: weak Tier B clubs can afford things again
- A new Tier B career starts with 1 scout instead of 3, which frees wage budget for a content creator.
- A Tier B club's weekly wage budget is never below $1,500.
- Tier B cup prize pools are 30% of the matching Open prize pool (were 20%).

## Notes for testers
