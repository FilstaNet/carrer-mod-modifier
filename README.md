# Carrer Mod Modifier
Adds new features and updates existing features to enhance gameplay for existing Carrer Mod by Nixus STA for rocket league.

# For unfamiliar with GitHub:
Go to `Releases` on right panel, and scroll down until you find `.exe` file. download it and follow the steps in the readme in the same releases page.

## Features - beta v0.6.3
* **Tier A and Tier B tournaments distinctions**: Tier B now has over **200** new real teams, and over **1000** new real players,
* **Promotions and Relegations**: Every season 2 worst teams in A-tier falls into Tier B. Top 1 team from Tier B promote to Tier A, and top 2-5 compete in single-elimination bracket. Winner promote to Tier A,
* **You can start as a Tier B team**: as a Tier B team the game gets longer - it takes more time to build strong squad,
* **Tier B cups**: Tier B teams cannot compete in Opens or RLCS. They have their own Tier B cups with prizepools, and points,
* **Academy**: For Tier A teams with enough money, Academy for training worse players. You get it when you either:
   - Place twice in top 12 on major
   - Place at least once in top 8 on major
   - qualify for world championship
* **Scrims**:
    * Setting up scrims in any format (3v3, 2v2, 1v1) with another team. It raises team morale, synergy and rating by little. You can only simulate 2v2 and 1v1 matches for now - they're not yet playable. You can set up at most 5 scrims every month. Clubs may decline your offer.

* **Club tab**:
    * **Invitationals** - You can now host your own tournaments after your club reaches 60,000 followers, with prizepool, sponsors, and invite teams.
    * **Showdowns** - Events with prizes. Lasts only one day - not playable.
    * **Chemistry** - When a squad plays together thei build synergy, and a new one has lower synergy. It matters in your matches - the better the synergy, the better the results
* **New tournaments** 
    * **Kick-off Weekend** - 3 day event a week before the first open. Regional, top 8 from last season.
    * **Esports World Cup** - Lasts for 6 days, happen between second major and LCQ; Top 16 A-tier clubs of the season from all regions.
    Both give you no ranking points, just a prize money.
* **Highly extended betting possibilities** (furtherly developed with the v0.5 update):
- **Combined Bets** - You can now combine bets to gain higher multiplier,
- **Parlays**
- **History tab** - Detailed history of bets.
* Every season more new young players join free agent pool (At the start of the season and mid-season transfer window).
* Refreshed tabs:
    * Standings,
    * notifications (inbox),
    * Advance,
    * Office media offers and betting,
    * Calendar
    * RLCS season overview,
    * Recent results, Stats, Honours and Season history,
    * Save generation screen (Tier B),
    * Player transfers,
    * Few other minor changes
* **Starring and archiving inbox mails,**
* **Prizepool is now shown on each event,**
* **New Y messages, keeping feed fresh,**
* **Openable board expectation window with details,**

* **Auto-update** - Implemented auto-update on launch, and by click of a button - you can choose to disable auto-update in setup.

* Minor fixes and quality-of-life updates.
